Traveling is not just moving from one point to another. Traveling is discovering, connecting, learning, and creating memories that stay with you forever.

With that idea, **ToursRed** was born: a Mexican platform created to connect travelers with local agencies, authentic experiences, and services that facilitate every stage of the journey.

This article marks the official start of the blog and also the beginning of a new chapter for our community.

![ToursRed Logo](/images/blog/logo.png)

---

## ✨ What is ToursRed?

ToursRed is a digital travel ecosystem that connects:

- **Travelers** seeking organized and reliable experiences
- **Agencies** looking to sell more and professionalize their digital presence
- **Strategic providers** such as insurance companies and wholesalers

In this first phase, we launch our **corporate website**, where we explain the project and enable initial services for both travelers and agencies.

## 🚀 A project built in stages

From the beginning, we decided to develop ToursRed in phases, prioritizing structure, legality, and scalability.

### 🟢 Phase 1 — Corporate Launch (Current)

You can now:

- ✓ Quote international travel with wholesale partners
- ✓ Purchase travel insurance
- ✓ Request personalized itineraries
- ✓ Receive advice to formalize your agency

The goal is to start building community while the main platform is completed.

![Map and travel planning](https://images.pexels.com/photos/1252500/pexels-photo-1252500.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1920)

---

### 🟡 Phase 2 — Tours Platform Launch (May–July 2026)

Here we will release the platform where different agencies can:

- Publish national tours
- Manage reservations
- Receive payments

Travelers will be able to:

- Explore experiences by destination
- Compare options
- Book online
- Access clear and detailed information

ToursRed will act as a technological intermediary, facilitating the connection process.

![Travelers exploring tourist destination](https://images.pexels.com/photos/1285625/pexels-photo-1285625.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1920)

---

### 🟠 Phase 3 — Direct International Travel with Agencies

In addition to working with wholesalers, we will open the platform for agencies that organize international travel on their own.

This will expand the global offering while maintaining the focus on real agencies.

![Airplane taking off at sunset](https://images.pexels.com/photos/358319/pexels-photo-358319.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1920)

---

### 🔵 Phase 4 — Mobile App

Later we will launch the mobile application to:

- Check reservations
- Receive reminders
- Access tour details
- Get more agile support

## 🌿 Coming Soon: Nature Stay Hub

![Nature Stay Hub](https://images.pexels.com/photos/2398220/pexels-photo-2398220.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=800)

As part of the ecosystem, **Nature Stay Hub** will arrive, a platform specialized in natural accommodations:

- Camping
- Glamping
- Cabins
- Eco-lodges
- Sustainable spaces

The idea is to connect conscious travelers with local hosts in natural environments.

![Glamping in nature](https://images.pexels.com/photos/6271549/pexels-photo-6271549.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1920)

*Nature Stay Hub is part of ToursRed's commitment to sustainable tourism and experiences in contact with nature.*
