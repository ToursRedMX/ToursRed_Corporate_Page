**The Mexican tourism experiences platform was selected among 13 entrepreneurial projects to present its business model to a panel of mentors, business leaders, and allies of the entrepreneurial ecosystem.**

**Mexico City, 13 October 2026.** – ToursRed, a Mexican technology platform focused on connecting travelers with travel agencies and tour operators, took part in the **Futura Capital 2026 Entrepreneurs Showcase**, held on 24 September in Pachuca, Hidalgo, at the Tecnológico de Monterrey campus.

The company was selected as one of the 13 projects participating in this edition, an opportunity to present its business proposal, share its vision for the digital transformation of tourism, and build connections with members of the business and entrepreneurial ecosystem.

## A technology proposal to transform how tourism experiences are sold

During its presentation, ToursRed shared its vision of developing a marketplace specialized in the distribution and sale of tours and tourism experiences, designed to make it easier for agencies, operators, and travelers to connect through a digital platform.

The project aims to contribute to the digitalization of Mexico's tourism sector by giving agencies and operators a new sales channel that lets them broaden their reach, increase their visibility, and bring their experiences to new markets.

For travelers, the proposal offers a single place where they can discover, compare, and book experiences offered by different tourism providers, making it easier to plan their next adventures.

With this model, ToursRed aims to strengthen the participation of small and medium-sized tourism businesses in the digital economy and foster new commercial opportunities across the industry.

## A space for learning, networking, and business growth

Taking part in Futura Capital 2026 was an opportunity to give the project visibility, share its growth goals, and receive feedback from mentors and representatives of the business sector.

![ToursRed representative holding the participation recognition at the Tecnológico de Monterrey](/images/press/FuturaCapital-Reconocimiento.jpg)

The Showcase brought together a range of entrepreneurial initiatives that presented their proposals, business models, and growth strategies, encouraging the exchange of ideas and the creation of connections between entrepreneurs and players in the innovation ecosystem.

For ToursRed, this event is part of a strategy aimed at strengthening its presence in Mexico's entrepreneurial ecosystem, developing strategic relationships, and laying the foundations for its growth.

![Official recognition awarded to ToursRed for its participation in the Futura Capital 2026 Showcase](/images/press/FuturaCapital-Constancia.jpg)

## Mexico as the starting point for a regional vision

ToursRed keeps its initial focus on the Mexican market, with the goal of building a network of travel agencies and tour operators that helps drive the sale of experiences nationwide.

In the long term, the company plans to expand its model to other Latin American markets, starting with Colombia, as part of its vision of connecting more travelers with authentic tourism experiences.

Taking part in initiatives such as Futura Capital is part of this path of development, learning, and connection with organizations that promote entrepreneurship and innovation.

## One more step in the ToursRed story

Being selected to take part in the Futura Capital 2026 Entrepreneurs Showcase is a milestone in ToursRed's development and an opportunity to keep strengthening its value proposition.

The company thanks the Futura Capital organizers, mentors, and participants for creating spaces that contribute to the growth of new business ventures.

ToursRed will keep working on technology solutions that help connect travelers with new experiences while creating growth opportunities for companies in the tourism sector.

### About ToursRed

**ToursRed** is a Mexican technology platform for the sale and distribution of tourism experiences, developed by **Tours Red Global S.A.S. de C.V.**

Its purpose is to connect travelers with travel agencies and tour operators through a digital marketplace that makes it easier to discover, promote, and book tours and experiences.

With a vision focused on innovation, accessibility, and the development of the tourism sector, ToursRed seeks to drive the digital transformation of the tourism industry in Mexico and, later, in Latin America.
