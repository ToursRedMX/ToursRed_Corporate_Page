**Mexico City, February 2026.**

ToursRed, a Mexican platform under development focused on the commercialization of tours and tourist experiences operated by local agencies, announces the obtainment of its **Tourism Registry with the Mexico City Secretary of Tourism** as a Travel Agency, marking a key step in the formal consolidation of the project.

This registry reaffirms ToursRed's commitment to legality, transparency, and the promotion of responsible tourism, establishing solid foundations for the growth of its digital ecosystem.

ToursRed was born with the objective of becoming a technological marketplace that directly connects travelers with local travel agencies, allowing the latter to publish and market their own tours, while users access authentic experiences in different destinations throughout Mexico.

*"From the beginning, we were clear that we wanted to build something serious, well-founded and aligned with tourism regulations. Obtaining this registry represents an important step within our roadmap,"* stated its founder.

## Strategic Alliances and First Phase of the Project

As part of this initial phase, ToursRed has established strategic alliances with international wholesalers and tourism service providers, which will allow offering from now on:

- International trips operated by wholesale partners
- Travel insurance sales
- Custom itinerary planning
- Transportation quote services

These solutions function as satellite services while the development of the main national tours platform is completed.

## Upcoming Phases

The project contemplates several stages of growth:

**Launch of the national tours digital platform**, where local agencies will be able to directly publish their experiences.

**Mobile application development.**

**Expansion toward international travel** operated directly by agencies.

**Launch of Nature Stay Hub**, a platform focused on natural accommodations such as glamping, cabins and eco-tourism spaces.

Each phase seeks to strengthen a technological ecosystem that benefits both travelers and tourism service providers.

## A Vision Made in Mexico

ToursRed is a 100% Mexican initiative that bets on local talent, sustainable tourism, and the use of technology to democratize access to authentic travel experiences.

With this tourism registry, the company takes another step toward its consolidation as a new player within the travel tech sector in Mexico.
