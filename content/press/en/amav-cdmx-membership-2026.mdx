**Mexico City, June 9, 2026.**

ToursRed proudly announces its official membership in the **Mexican Association of Travel Agencies of Mexico City (AMAV CDMX)**, one of the most representative and recognized organizations in the Mexican tourism sector.

With this affiliation, ToursRed joins a community of companies committed to the professionalization, development, and strengthening of the national tourism industry, reaffirming its commitment to quality, formality, and innovation for the benefit of travelers, travel agencies, and tour operators.

![AMAV CDMX Membership Certificate 2026-2027](/images/press/Constancia.jpeg)

## Another Step in ToursRed's Consolidation

The AMAV CDMX membership represents a significant milestone in ToursRed's growth and adds to other institutional achievements recently reached, including:

* Active National Tourism Registry (RNT) granted by the Ministry of Tourism.
* Mexico City Tourism Registry Certificate.
* "ToursRed" trademark registered with the Mexican Institute of Industrial Property (IMPI).
* Legal incorporation of the company as TOURS RED GLOBAL S.A.S. DE C.V.

These achievements strengthen the confidence of travelers, agencies, and commercial partners, consolidating ToursRed as a company committed to best practices in the tourism sector.

![ToursRed listed in the official AMAV CDMX member directory](/images/press/PaginaAMAV-ToursRed.jpg)

## Technology at the Service of Tourism

ToursRed was founded with the mission of connecting travelers with verified travel agencies, facilitating the search, comparison, and booking of tours, excursions, and tourist experiences through a technology platform designed to drive tourism commerce in Mexico.

In addition to bringing new opportunities to travelers, the platform aims to support travel agencies through digital tools that allow them to expand their reach, increase their visibility, and strengthen their online presence.

## A Commitment to Mexican Tourism

From the very beginning, ToursRed has maintained a close relationship with the promotion of domestic tourism. As part of this vision, its founder, **Alan Axel Álvarez Hernández**, has traveled through all 32 states of the Mexican Republic and visited all 177 Pueblos Mágicos (Magic Towns) of the country, gaining extensive knowledge of the tourist destinations that make up Mexico's cultural, natural, and historical wealth.

This experience has helped shape the philosophy of ToursRed: promoting authentic experiences, driving responsible tourism, and contributing to the sustainable growth of the sector.

## Looking to the Future

Joining AMAV CDMX marks the beginning of a new chapter for ToursRed. The company will continue working on the development of new technological tools, strategic alliances, and innovative services that generate value for travelers, travel agencies, tour operators, and sector providers.

We thank AMAV CDMX for the trust they have placed in us and reiterate our commitment to continuing to build a stronger, more collaborative, and more accessible tourism ecosystem for everyone.

*Because the best stories begin with a tour.*
